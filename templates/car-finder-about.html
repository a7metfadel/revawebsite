{% trans "Our Story" %}

{% trans "Under exceptional circumstances imposed by the war, the idea of “Reva Pharma” was born in 2014, on the initiative of a group of academics and specialists with extensive experience in the pharmaceutical industry. The company was established in direct response to an urgent national need to provide essential medicines, especially pediatric medicines, amidst a harsh health environment that suffered from a severe shortage of medicines." %}

{% trans "The launch of Reva Pharma was not just an investment project, but an integrated health and development initiative based on the conviction that healthcare is a human right. Since its inception, the company has been committed to applying the highest international quality standards in pharmaceutical manufacturing, putting patient safety and treatment effectiveness at the forefront of its priorities." %}

{% trans "The company started by producing a range of essential and vital medicines, then gradually expanded to include a wide range of pharmaceutical varieties that meet the needs of the local market. Today, after more than a decade of continuous work, Reva Pharma's portfolio includes more than 200 pharmaceutical products, with ambitious plans to reach 400 by 2028. The company is home to a dedicated team of more than 300 employees who share a common commitment to realizing the company's vision of becoming a leader in the pharmaceutical industry." %}

{% trans "Because community development is an integral part of our responsibility, Reva Pharma has established the" %} {% trans "Maarif University of Applied Sciences" %} {% trans "to develop specialized scientific competencies that meet the needs of the pharmaceutical market and contribute to building the future. It also launched the" %} {% trans "Knowledge School" %} {% trans "to provide an integrated learning environment for children, believing that investing in education and knowledge is the cornerstone of the renaissance of societies." %}

{% trans "At Reva Pharma, we believe that the pharmaceutical industry goes beyond production to being a humanitarian and scientific mission. We aspire to be a landmark in the health sector and a leading pharmaceutical name locally and regionally, contributing to improving the quality of human life and leaving a real footprint in the march of healthcare." %}
